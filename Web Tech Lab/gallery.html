Our Achievements

GROWTH AFRICA ACCELERATOR PROGRAM - 2022 COHORT

ExpressMed Healthcare was selected to be part of Growth Africa's 6-month intensive startup acceleration program. We were among 75+ cohort members, joining entrepreneurs from Ghana, Kenya, Ethiopia, Gambia, and Uganda. The program selected startups with ideas that impact the community and have high growth potential. Throughout the program, our staff engaged in various teamwork sessions focused on product-solution fit, value architecture, gap analysis, growth planning, and budgeting.

GHANA DIGITAL INNOVATION WEEK

We participated in Ghana's Digital Innovation Week to introduce our services, especially our technology platform, to potential clients and partners. We showcased our telehealth solutions, mobile labs, medicine delivery, and other innovative healthcare services. This event was part of a trilateral program celebrating Ghana's digital innovation ecosystem and building momentum towards a common vision for its future.

COMMUNITY HEALTH INITIATIVES

Our community health initiatives have reached thousands of people across various regions in Ghana. These programs focus on preventive healthcare, health education, and improving access to healthcare services in underserved communities.

We regularly organize health outreach programs in various communities to provide free health screenings, consultations, and health education. These programs help us reach underserved populations and make healthcare more accessible to all.

Our corporate wellness workshops help organizations promote employee health and wellbeing. These workshops cover various topics including stress management, healthy eating, physical activity, and preventive healthcare.
