ABOUT US

ExpressMed is a health tech company providing convenient and accessible preventive healthcare to Ghanaians in the comfort of their homes. As purveyors of preventive medicine, we emphasize prevention efforts, early disease identification through screening, and the treatment of both acute and chronic illnesses. Since its establishment in 2016, ExpressMed has gradually become the best telehealth provider and homecare manager for elderly patients with chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, and stroke.

ExpressMed Healthcare works with over 30 general and specialist doctors, 35 nurses, 5 laboratory and diagnostic facilities, 7 dieticians, and 5 physiotherapists and fitness experts across Ghana. Additionally, we work closely with a lot of hospitals to ensure a comprehensive wellness program for our patients. To guarantee the delivery of high-quality services, all employees and partners undergo thorough screening, verification, and training.
