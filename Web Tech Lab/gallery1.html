EXPRESSMED TAKES PART IN GHANA DIGITAL INNOVATION WEEK (GDIW) 2022

ExpressMed Healthcare joined other innovative businesses in Ghana and Africa to showcase their products and services during a 3-day exhibition in Accra. We used the opportunity to introduce our services, especially our technology platform, to potential clients and partners. Among the services we presented include telehealth, mobile labs, medicine delivery, doctor consultation at home, nursing home care, and corporate wellness program. We continue to engage our clients and potential clients through events to better understand their needs. A trilateral program to celebrate the accomplishments of Ghana's digital innovation ecosystem and build momentum towards a common vision for its future.

GROWTH AFRICA ACCELERATOR PROGRAM - 2022 COHORT

ExpressMed Healthcare was selected to be part of Growth Africa's 6-month intensive startup acceleration program. We were among 75+ cohort members, joining entrepreneurs from Ghana, Kenya, Ethiopia, Gambia, and Uganda. The program selected startups with ideas that impact the community and have high growth potential. Throughout the program, our staff engaged in various teamwork sessions focused on product-solution fit, value architecture, gap analysis, growth planning, and budgeting.
